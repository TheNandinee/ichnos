    What we can't claim

    • Labels are time windows: Ground-truth evaluation labels denote known interference time windows rather than per-packet emitter truth.
    • ADS-B under-counts jamming: Severe RF jamming causes receivers or transponders to drop position packets entirely, creating silence gaps rather than degraded NIC/NACp fields.
    • No military aircraft coverage: Public crowdsourced ADS-B feeds exclude encrypted military transponders and tactical datalinks.

    DETECTION ARCHITECTURE

    Two-Lane Passive Detection

    Lane 1 (Integrity & Jamming) aggregates Navigation Integrity Category (NIC) and Navigation Accuracy Category (NACp) degradations across H3 hexagonal cells to map spatial radio-frequency interference. Lane 2 (Kinematic Spoofing) flags aircraft trajectories exhibiting position reports that imply physically impossible speeds and also fail two track-consistency tests (neighbour-interpolation residual and predecessor gap) even when integrity flags remain nominal. Spatiotemporal DBSCAN clustering groups co-occurring anomalies into discrete events governed by a minimum aircraft-report threshold (MinPts). For each clustered event, radio horizon geometry intersects affected aircraft altitudes to bound a feasible region where a ground-based transmitter could maintain line of sight.
