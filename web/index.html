HOW IT WORKS

Listen. Detect. Bound. Prove.

01 · LISTEN

Every aircraft is a sensor

Airliners broadcast their GPS position about once a second. ICHNOS reads open archives and its own receive-only antennas. It never transmits.

02 · DETECT

Two lanes, two attacks

Lane 1 maps integrity drops: jamming. Lane 2 flags position jumps that imply impossible speeds and fail two track-consistency tests: spoofing that integrity maps cannot see.

03 · BOUND

Physics narrows the search

A ground transmitter must be within the radio horizon of every affected aircraft. Intersecting those horizons gives a feasible region for investigators, never a false "fix".

04 · PROVE

An open benchmark

Results are scored against dated, cited Indian incident windows and quiet control days, with confidence intervals and false-alarm bounds. Anyone can replay them.

What we don't claim

  • Recall ties at this sample size. Integrity-only and ICHNOS detect the same labelled windows. The added value is telling spoofing from jamming and bounding the source.
  • Labels are time windows from public reports, not exact events. Control days may contain real interference.
  • ADS-B under-counts where receiver coverage is thin, as near the border. That is why ground nodes are on the roadmap.
  • No location fixes. Physics gives a feasible region. Exact direction-finding is the Wireless Monitoring Organisation's job.
