A standard map sees jamming. ICHNOS also sees spoofing.

Integrity-based maps colour Delhi orange and stop there. ICHNOS also checks two things a spoofer cannot fake, how fast an airliner can really move and the air pressure outside it, and tracks them every day across a year.

How it works

Listen. Detect. Bound. Prove.

Four steps, all on public data, all receive-only, all replayable.

01 · LISTEN

Every aircraft is a sensor

Airliners broadcast their GPS position about once a second. ICHNOS reads verified open archives and never transmits anything.

02 · DETECT

Three lanes, two attacks

Lane 1 flags integrity drops in aircraft that are normally healthy: jamming. Lanes 2 and 3 flag impossible jumps and GPS-vs-pressure altitude mismatches: spoofing.

03 · BOUND

Physics narrows the search

A ground transmitter must be within line of sight of every affected aircraft. Tracks that cross a jammed zone give a centre estimate with an error radius.

04 · PROVE

An open benchmark

Every lane is scored on IN-RFI-Replay v1: 14 cited Indian windows and a year of sampled days, with confidence intervals. Coverage gaps are reported, never hidden.

What we don't claim

  • Overlapping intervals. The lanes are not proven better than integrity-only maps, only comparable and slightly ahead.
  • One scorable region. Only Delhi has dated labels, and its six windows overlap.
  • Coverage changes. With the border receiver held constant, the Jan–Feb 2026 jamming signal is inconclusive (AUC 0.69, p 0.08). The reported November week survives that control.
  • Simulation gap. The learned model learns injected attacks that do not yet look like real spoofing.
  • No location fixes. Feasible regions and estimates with error radii only. Precise border estimates are never published.
Platform

Infrastructure, not a dashboard.

The map is one client. Underneath is a data layer that airlines, regulators, defence users and drone makers can build on.

  • Replay APIEvents with window, aircraft count, lane, threshold and feasible region, over REST.
  • Sovereign by designOne Docker command, runs air-gapped. Public tier coarsened; precise estimates stay in a restricted tier.
  • Open benchmarkIN-RFI-Replay v1: 14 cited windows, a public scorer and a leaderboard any vendor can enter.
  • ₹20k ground nodesReceive-only receivers to make border coverage continuous instead of one volunteer feed that comes and goes.
