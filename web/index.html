How it works

Listen. Detect. Bound. Prove.

Four steps, all on public data, all receive-only, all replayable.

01 · LISTEN

Every aircraft is a sensor

Airliners broadcast their GPS position about once a second. ICHNOS reads open archives and its own receive-only antennas.

02 · DETECT

Two lanes, two attacks

Lane 1 maps integrity drops: jamming. Lane 2 flags jumps that imply impossible speeds and fail two track tests: spoofing.

03 · BOUND

Physics narrows the search

A ground transmitter must be within the radio horizon of every affected aircraft. Intersecting them gives a feasible region.

04 · PROVE

An open benchmark

Scored against dated, cited Indian incident windows and control days, with confidence intervals and false-alarm bounds.

What we don't claim

  • Recall ties at this sample size. Integrity-only and ICHNOS detect the same labelled windows. The added value is telling spoofing from jamming and bounding the source.
  • Labels are time windows from public reports, not exact events. Control days may contain real interference.
  • ADS-B under-counts where receiver coverage is thin, as near the border. Ground nodes close that gap.
  • No location fixes. Physics gives a feasible region. Direction-finding is the Wireless Monitoring Organisation's job.
