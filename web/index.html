THE PROBLEM

India's navigation layer is under attack.

Jamming blocks satellite signals. Spoofing fakes them. Both are now officially on the record over Indian airspace.

THE BLIND SPOT

Standard maps miss spoofing.

Public interference maps read each aircraft's own integrity value. Spoofing can keep that value healthy while the reported position jumps somewhere impossible. So the map stays green.

ICHNOS adds a second lane that checks the physics of every track: no airliner moves hundreds of metres sideways in a second.

Research: over 99% of confirmed spoofing anomalies kept integrity indicators high (Park, Lee & Son, arXiv 2607.09700, 2026).

HOW IT WORKS

Listen. Detect. Bound. Prove.

01 · LISTEN

Aircraft as sensors

Every airliner broadcasts its GPS position about once a second. ICHNOS reads open archives and rooftop receivers. It never transmits.

02 · DETECT

Two lanes

Lane 1 watches integrity drops (jamming). Lane 2 flags physically impossible jumps that pass track-consistency tests (spoofing).

03 · BOUND

Radio physics

A ground transmitter must be within line of sight of every affected aircraft. Intersecting those horizons gives a feasible region, never a false "fix".

04 · PROVE

Open benchmark

Results are scored against dated, cited Indian incidents, with confidence intervals and false-alarm bounds. Anyone can replay them.
